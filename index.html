The Solution

Meet LocalCast. Your room is the network.

LocalCast turns the WiFi already in your room into a private broadcast channel. Three steps, zero friction:

1

Start LocalCast

Open the app on your laptop. It instantly creates a presentation room on your local network.

2

Share a local link

Attendees type one short address into any browser. That's it — no app, no account, no internet.

3

Everyone follows live

Your slides or live screen appear on every phone, tablet, and laptop in the room — in real time.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Does it work without internet?
Yes — that's the whole point. LocalCast runs entirely over your local WiFi network. No router with internet? Use Hotspot Mode and your laptop becomes the network itself.
Do attendees need to install anything?
No. Attendees simply open their browser and enter a short local address. No apps, no accounts, no downloads — they're watching in seconds.
Which devices are supported?
Any device with a modern browser: iPhones, Android phones, tablets, laptops, and Chromebooks. Presenters run LocalCast on Windows, macOS, or Linux.
Is LocalCast free?
Early access members get LocalCast free during the beta, plus a generous discount at launch. A free tier for small classrooms is planned.
